**It is probably a mouse if:**

## Why Sealing Matters as Much as Removal

Removing the rodents you can see solves only half the problem. If the gaps stay open, new rodents will find them, often within weeks. That is why our service ends with exclusion, not with the last trap.

The order also matters. Openings should be sealed after the rodents are cleared out, or you risk trapping them inside your walls. Our technicians plan the work in the right order so you do not have to.

## When to Call a Professional

You can try to handle a single mouse on your own. Call us if:

* You see signs in more than one room or level of the house
* The noises or droppings continue after a week or two of your own traps
* You suspect rats, since they are harder to trap and often come from outside burrows
* Someone in the home has asthma, allergies or a weakened immune system
* You have pets or young children and want safe placement of traps
* You are buying, selling or renting out the home and need the problem cleared

## Rodent Season in Downers Grove

Rodent calls rise when the weather turns cold. Mice and rats move indoors in fall, and activity stays high through winter. Rats can also be active around yards and outbuildings in spring and summer, especially near bird feeders, garbage and compost.

The best time to get ahead of rodents is early fall, before the first cold nights. If you are already seeing signs, do not wait for a better season. Rodent problems only grow.
