## Why Big Homes Have Big Pest Challenges

* **Tall rooflines and many eaves.** Wasp and hornet nests often start high up, out of sight. Large two and three story homes have plenty of overhangs, soffits and gables where nests can grow.
* **Large attics and crawl spaces.** More hidden space gives mice and other pests room to settle before you notice. Droppings or noises may show up long after they arrive.
* **Historic wood and detailing.** Older homes with wood trim, porches and pergolas attract carpenter bees. Wood that stays damp attracts carpenter ants and termites.
* **Mature trees and wooded lots.** Trees shelter ticks, mosquitoes and carpenter ant colonies near the house.
* **Finished basements and older foundations.** Moisture problems bring silverfish, centipedes and sometimes cockroaches.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do large homes really have more pest problems?**
Not always more, but they are harder to watch. More attic, crawl space and roofline means pests can settle in unnoticed. Regular exterior treatments and inspections catch problems earlier.

**Are historic Hinsdale homes at higher risk for termites?**
Older homes with wood framing, basements or crawl spaces and past moisture problems can be. A termite inspection before buying or renovating gives you a clear picture. See [Termite Inspection](/termite-inspection-downers-grove-il).

**Why are there wasp nests high on my home?**
Wasps and hornets pick sheltered spots, such as soffits, gables and deep eaves, and tall homes have many of them. Nests begin small in spring and grow through summer. Do not try to remove nests from a ladder. See [Wasp and Hornet Nest Removal](/wasp-hornet-nest-removal-downers-grove-il).

**Will carpenter bees damage my porch and trim?**
They can. Carpenter bees drill round holes in bare or weathered softwood such as porch ceilings, railings, pergolas and eaves. Treating the tunnels and protecting the wood helps. See [Carpenter Bee Control](/carpenter-bee-control-downers-grove-il).

**How do I check a large attic for mice?**
Look for droppings, gnawed insulation and nests, and listen for scratching at night. If you find signs, call us for an inspection and sealing plan. See [Mouse Control](/mouse-control-downers-grove-il).
