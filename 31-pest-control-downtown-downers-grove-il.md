## A Dense, Older Core

Downtown sits on some of the oldest blocks in the village, close to the original railroad grid that shaped how Downers Grove grew. Buildings here mix ground floor retail and restaurants with condos and apartments above, and many structures carry decades of age. That combination brings its own pest pattern.

**Restaurants and food businesses** draw cockroaches, mice and flies if drains, storage areas and trash are not managed carefully, and pests from one unit can move into the apartments above or next door.

**Condos and apartments above shops** can see cockroaches, bed bugs and mice travel through shared walls, floors and utility chases, so a problem in one unit rarely stays contained to that unit.

**Older buildings** have more gaps around old window frames, transoms, utility penetrations and foundation joints, which is exactly what ants, spiders and mice look for.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**I live in a condo above a downtown restaurant. Can pests come from the restaurant?**
Yes. Cockroaches and mice can travel up through shared walls, pipe chases and utility openings between a ground floor restaurant and the units above it. If you are seeing pests in an upstairs condo, the source may be several floors away.

**Why do older downtown buildings get more pests?**
Age brings more small gaps: around old window and door frames, foundation joints, and places where pipes and wires pass through walls. Those gaps are entry points for ants, mice and spiders that newer construction does not have as often.
