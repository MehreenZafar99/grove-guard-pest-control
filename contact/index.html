What Happens After You Contact Us

  1. We review your request. We look at the pest, the location and how long it has been going on.
  2. We call or text you. We ask a few short questions and set a time that works for you.
  3. A technician inspects. They find the pest, find how it is getting in, and explain what they see.
  4. You get the price first. We tell you what the treatment includes and what it costs before any work begins.

Tell Us These Things for a Faster Answer

  • What kind of pest you think it is, or what it looks like
  • Which room or part of the yard it is in
  • How long you have seen it
  • If you have pets or young children at home
  • If it is a house, townhome, condo, rental or business

If you are not sure what pest you have, that is fine. A photo helps, and we can identify it for you.
