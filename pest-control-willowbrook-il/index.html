A Village Named for Its Willows

Willowbrook began when residents of the Ridgemoor subdivision decided to incorporate in 1960, when the village had only 167 people in 37 houses. It was named after the willow trees along a creek behind one resident's property, and that creek flows toward Waterfall Glen Forest Preserve. Route 83 later became a busy corridor of shopping centers and hotels.

Today the village has ten community parks, Lake Hinsdale Park, creeks, ponds and stormwater detention basins. Water is a big part of the landscape, and it is also the reason mosquitoes are a common call here.

Water Nearby Means Mosquitoes and Ants

Creeks, detention basins and ponds hold standing or slow moving water, especially after heavy rain. Mosquitoes breed in that water and rest in the shade of nearby shrubs and grass. Yards next to ponds or basins may see more of them than homes farther away. Wet ground and thick landscaping can also attract ants and earwigs.

Waterfall Glen Forest Preserve sits nearby, which brings ticks and rodents toward some yards as well.

Houses, Condos and Apartments

Willowbrook has traditional single family neighborhoods and several apartment and condo communities. The pests you see depend on where you live:

  • Single family homes. Mice in fall and winter, ants in kitchens, spiders in garages and basements, and mosquitoes and ticks in yards.
  • Condos and apartments. Cockroaches, bed bugs and mice can move between units through shared walls, pipes and ceilings. Treating one unit is not always enough, so we look for the source.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there a lot of mosquitoes near Willowbrook ponds and basins?

Standing water is where mosquitoes breed, so yards near ponds, creeks and detention basins can see more. Dump standing water on your property weekly, clear gutters and consider treating shady resting areas. See Mosquito Control.

I live in a Willowbrook apartment and see roaches. What do I do?

Tell your property manager and call us for an inspection. Roaches travel through walls and pipes, so treatment works best when the source is found. See Cockroach Control.

How do bed bugs spread in condo buildings?

They ride in on luggage, clothing, furniture and visitors, and can also travel through wall gaps and pipe openings between units. Prompt treatment and preparation matter. See Bed Bug Treatment.

Does Waterfall Glen bring ticks and rodents to Willowbrook?

It can for yards that sit close to open land. Ticks and rodents move with wildlife, and homes near wooded edges may see more. Yard treatment and sealing gaps in your home help.

Do mice come into single family homes in Willowbrook?

Yes, mostly in fall and winter when temperatures drop. Sealing gaps around foundations, garage doors and utility lines is key. See Mouse Control.
