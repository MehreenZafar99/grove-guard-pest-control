## Not Sure What Pest You Have?

Here are a few common clues.

* **Small dark droppings in cupboards or along walls:** often mice. See [Mouse Control](/mouse-control-downers-grove-il).
* **Fine sawdust piles near wood or window frames:** possibly carpenter ants. See [Carpenter Ant Control](/carpenter-ant-control-downers-grove-il).
* **Winged insects or shed wings near windows in spring:** could be termites or ants. Call for an [inspection](/termite-inspection-downers-grove-il).
* **Scratching in walls or the attic at night:** mice or rats. See [Rodent Control](/rodent-control-downers-grove-il).
* **Itchy bites in the morning and dark spots on mattress seams:** possibly bed bugs. See [Bed Bug Treatment](/bed-bug-treatment-downers-grove-il).
* **A papery nest under the eaves or in the ground:** wasps, hornets or yellow jackets. See [Wasp and Hornet Nest Removal](/wasp-hornet-nest-removal-downers-grove-il).

If you still are not sure, send us a photo through the [contact page](/contact). We will help you identify it.
