## What the Trees Bring

**Carpenter ants.** These ants nest in dead trees, stumps, wet wood and damaged wood, then send workers into nearby homes. If you see large black ants after dark, or sawdust piles near window frames and trim, a nest may be close. See [Carpenter Ant Control](/carpenter-ant-control-downers-grove-il).

**Ticks and mosquitoes.** Leaf litter, shady ground cover and wooded edges are where ticks wait. Shade and damp ground also help mosquitoes rest and breed.

**Fall invaders.** Boxelder bugs feed on the seeds of boxelder, maple and ash trees, and both boxelder bugs and stink bugs gather on sunny walls in fall. Asian lady beetles do the same. All three can end up inside your home over winter.

**Wood damage.** Bare, weathered wood on decks, eaves and porch trim attracts carpenter bees, and any wood that stays damp can attract termites.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why do I have carpenter ants when I live near so many trees?**
Carpenter ants nest in dead or wet wood, including trees, stumps and wood in your home. Lisle has many mature trees, so a nest may be outside, with ants traveling into your house. Finding and treating the nest is the fix.

**Are ticks a problem in Lisle?**
They can be, particularly in yards with leaf litter, tall grass and shady wooded edges. Raking leaves, mowing and treating the tick zones of your yard lowers the risk. Check yourself and pets after time outdoors.

**When should I treat for boxelder bugs and stink bugs?**
Late summer to early fall, before they gather on your walls, gives the best results. Once they are inside your walls and attic, they are much harder to remove.

**Will my trees make mosquitoes worse?**
Shade and damp ground help mosquitoes rest during the day, so dense trees and shrubs can raise numbers. Dump standing water weekly and consider treating the shady resting areas of your yard.

**Do carpenter bees damage Lisle homes?**
They can. Carpenter bees drill round holes in bare or weathered wood, such as eaves, decks and porch trim. Treating the tunnels and protecting the wood helps stop the damage.
