Close Neighbors, Shared Pest Problems

Westmont borders Downers Grove to the west, Clarendon Hills to the east, Darien to the south and Oak Brook to the north. With more people per square mile than most nearby towns, the village has tight lots and homes built close to one another.

That matters for pests. Mice and rats can travel along fence lines and between yards. Cockroaches and ants can move through shared walls and utility lines. A problem next door often becomes your problem if the reasons pests are there are not fixed.

Cottages and Brick Homes of Older Westmont

The village was incorporated in 1921 and grew from small, modest cottages in early subdivisions. After World War II, brick homes became popular in developments such as Blackhawk Heights. Many of these older homes have crawl spaces, basements, aging foundations and wood framing that termites and carpenter ants can target.

Older homes also tend to have more gaps around pipes, vents and foundations, which is where mice look for a way in. Basements with moisture can attract silverfish, centipedes and cockroaches.

Apartments and Condos

Westmont has a long history of renters, and many residents live in apartments and condo buildings. In multi unit buildings, cockroaches, bed bugs and mice can spread between homes. Your treatment works best when the source and the path are found, not only the unit where you noticed the pest.

Near Ty Warner Park

Parks like Ty Warner Park, with its fishing lake, and Muddy Waters Park give residents green space in a compact village. Standing water and shaded areas nearby also give mosquitoes a place to breed and rest, so yards close to the park may see more of them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do pests spread so easily between Westmont homes?

Homes and yards sit close together, so mice, rats, ants and roaches can travel along fences, utility lines and shared walls. Fixing entry points and the things that attract pests on your property helps stop the cycle.

I rent an apartment in Westmont and have roaches. What should I do?

Tell your property manager right away and call us to inspect. Roaches spread through walls and pipes, so treatment works best when the source is found, not just the visible roaches. See Cockroach Control.

Are older Westmont homes more likely to get termites?

Older homes with wood framing, basements or crawl spaces and moisture problems can be at greater risk. A termite inspection is a smart step, especially before you buy, sell or renovate. See Termite Inspection.

How can I keep mice out of a closely packed neighborhood?

Seal gaps around pipes, vents, garage doors and the foundation, store food and pet food in sealed containers, and keep trash cans closed. If you already have mice, call us for removal and sealing. See Mouse Control.

Do I need mosquito control if I live near Ty Warner Park?

Yards close to ponds or lakes can see more mosquitoes. Dump standing water weekly, and consider a treatment for the shady spots where mosquitoes rest.
