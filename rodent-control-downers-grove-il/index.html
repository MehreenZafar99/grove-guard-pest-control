Mouse or Rat? Start Here

It is probably a mouse if: you see small droppings the size of a grain of rice, hear light scratching in walls at night, or find chewed food in the kitchen or pantry.

See our full guide to Mouse Control.

It is probably a rat if: the droppings are much larger, you see burrow holes along the foundation or under a deck or shed, or the gnaw marks are big and deep.

What Our Rodent Control Service Includes

Every rodent job follows the same five stages.

  • Inspection: We check the inside and outside of your home and yard to find the rodent, its path and its way in.
  • Removal: We remove the rodents using methods that fit your property and household.
  • Exclusion: We seal the openings so new rodents cannot get in.
  • Prevention advice: We point out what is drawing rodents to your property.
  • Follow up: We confirm that activity has stopped.

Why Sealing Matters as Much as Removal

Removing the rodents you can see solves only half the problem. If the gaps stay open, new rodents will find them, often within weeks. That is why our service ends with exclusion, not with the last trap. Denser Chicago-area properties can see more rodent pressure because neighboring buildings, yards and utility paths sit closer together.

The order also matters. Openings should be sealed after the rodents are cleared out, or you risk trapping them inside your walls. Our technicians plan the work in the right order so you do not have to. The typical Downers Grove home was built around 1974, so foundation joints and openings around brick, vinyl or aluminum exteriors can all need attention.

When to Call a Professional

You can try to handle a single mouse on your own. Call us if:

  • You see signs in more than one room or level of the house
  • The noises or droppings continue after a week or two of your own traps
  • You suspect rats, since they are harder to trap and often come from outside burrows
  • Someone in the home has asthma, allergies or a weakened immune system
  • You have pets or young children and want safe placement of traps
  • You are buying, selling or renting out the home and need the problem cleared

Rodent Season in Downers Grove

Rodent calls rise when the weather turns cold. Mice and rats move indoors in fall, and activity stays high through winter. Rats can also be active around yards and outbuildings in spring and summer, especially near bird feeders, garbage and compost. The first frost usually arrives in mid-to-late October, which makes early fall a useful time to check entry points.

The best time to get ahead of rodents is early fall, before the first cold nights. If you are already seeing signs, do not wait for a better season. Rodent problems only grow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do mice and rats need different treatments?

Yes. Mice are small, live inside walls and use tiny gaps. Rats are larger, often live in burrows outside and are more careful around new objects. The traps, placement and sealing work all differ, which is why each gets its own plan.

How much does rodent control cost?

The price depends on the type of rodent, how far the problem has spread, the size of the home and how much sealing is needed. We give a free estimate and explain the price before we start.

Do I need to leave my home during service?

Usually not. Traps and any products are placed in areas out of reach of children and pets. Your technician will explain the plan before starting.

How soon can you come out?

Call (630) 555-0184 and we will do our best to schedule an inspection as soon as possible. Rodent calls are treated as urgent.

Do you remove squirrels, raccoons or bats?

No. Those animals need a licensed wildlife control operator. We handle mice and rats.

Is there a best time of year to deal with rodents?

Early fall is ideal because you can stop rodents before they move in for winter. But if you have signs now, act now. Waiting only gives them time to breed and cause more damage.

Can I get ongoing rodent protection?

Yes. Our Home Pest Protection Plan includes regular visits that help keep rodents out through the year.
