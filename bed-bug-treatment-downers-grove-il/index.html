Having Bed Bugs Does Not Mean Your Home Is Dirty

Bed bugs feed on blood and travel with people. They can show up in clean homes and expensive homes alike. They are not a sign of poor housekeeping, and there is no reason to be embarrassed. Our job is simply to get rid of them.

Signs of Bed Bugs

Bites alone are not a reliable sign. Some people react strongly, and others do not react at all. Look for these signs instead:

  • Small reddish or rust colored spots on sheets or the mattress
  • Tiny dark dots, like ink marks, along mattress seams and the headboard
  • Pale yellow shed skins
  • Very small white eggs in seams and cracks
  • Live bugs, flat and reddish brown, about the size of an apple seed
  • A sweet, musty smell in heavy cases

Where they hide: mattress seams, box springs, bed frames and headboards, behind picture frames, in nightstands, along baseboards, in sofa seams and even inside outlet covers. They stay close to where people sleep or rest.

How Bed Bugs Get Into a Home

Bed bugs are hitchhikers. They ride into homes on:

  • Luggage and clothing after travel
  • Used furniture, mattresses and couches
  • Bags, backpacks and laundry
  • Visitors, or items you bring from other places

The Chicago area has a lot of people traveling in and out, which means bed bugs move with them. If you live in a townhome, duplex or condo and share a wall with a neighbor, bed bugs can also travel through wall gaps and pipes.

How We Treat Bed Bugs

1. Inspection. We check the bed, furniture and surrounding areas to find every place the bugs are living. This shows us how big the problem is and which rooms are affected.

2. A plan and a price. We explain what we found and how we will treat it.

3. Treatment. We treat the bed, furniture, baseboards and other hiding spots. Treatment may take a few hours, depending on how many rooms are affected.

4. Follow up visits. Bed bug eggs hatch about a week after they are laid, so one visit is rarely enough. We come back to treat new bugs that hatch and confirm the problem is gone.

How to Prepare for Your Treatment

Good preparation makes treatment work better. Your technician will give you exact steps, but here is the general idea.

  • Wash bedding, curtains and clothes from the affected rooms, then dry them on high heat for at least 30 minutes
  • Place clean items in sealed plastic bags until treatment is done
  • Reduce clutter near the bed so we can reach the hiding spots
  • Vacuum the mattress, bed frame, floors and furniture, then take the vacuum contents outside in a sealed bag
  • Do not move items from the affected room to other rooms
  • Do not throw away your mattress or furniture unless we tell you to

Mistakes That Make Bed Bugs Worse

Using bug bombs. Foggers do not reach deep hiding spots and can scatter bed bugs into other rooms and walls.

Spraying store bought products. Many do not work on bed bugs, and some make the bugs spread.

Moving everything around. Carrying items from room to room helps bed bugs spread.

Throwing out furniture too soon. Bed bugs can be treated on most furniture, and throwing it out does not remove them from the rest of the home.

Waiting. Bed bugs multiply, and each week the problem gets bigger and harder to treat.

Are Bed Bugs Dangerous?

Bed bugs are not known to spread disease. The main problems are itchy bites, loss of sleep and stress. Some people scratch until the skin is irritated, which can lead to infection. If you have a strong reaction to bites, talk to your doctor.

Frequently Asked Questions

How did I get bed bugs?

Most likely from travel, used furniture or a visitor. Bed bugs hide in luggage, clothing and furniture, then move into your bed. It can be hard to know exactly when or where it happened.

How long does it take to get rid of bed bugs?

Most homes need more than one visit over a few weeks. Eggs hatch after treatment, so follow up visits are what finish the job. We explain the schedule before we start.

Do I need to throw out my mattress?

Usually not. Mattresses and furniture can be treated. A bed bug proof cover on the mattress and box spring can also help trap any bugs left inside.

Can I stay in my home during treatment?

In most cases, yes, but you may need to stay out of treated rooms for a few hours. Your technician will tell you what to expect.

Will bug bombs or sprays from the store work?

Usually not. They rarely reach where bed bugs hide and can push them deeper into walls or into other rooms, which makes treatment harder.

Is bed bug treatment safe for children and pets?

Your technician follows label directions and tells you which areas to avoid and for how long. Let us know about children, pets and anyone with health concerns before we start.

How can I tell if it is bed bugs or something else?

Bites can look like other insect bites, so the bugs and their signs are the best proof. Look for dark spots, shed skins and live bugs on the mattress and bed frame. If you are not sure, we can inspect.

How much does bed bug treatment cost?

The price depends on how many rooms are affected, the size of the home, the method used and the number of visits. We give a free estimate and explain the price before we start.
