Why Pest Problems Differ Across Naperville

Naperville is the oldest town in DuPage County, settled in 1831. It has grown from about 13,000 residents in 1960 to nearly 150,000 today. That growth means homes of every age, from historic houses near downtown to neighborhoods built in recent decades. The city also sits in both DuPage and Will counties.

Nature adds another layer. The DuPage River runs through the city, and the Riverwalk follows it. Forest preserves such as Springbrook Prairie, McDowell Grove and Greene Valley sit along the edges, and the Naperville Park District looks after more than 2,200 acres of parks and open space. Water, shade and open land all shape which pests show up at your door.

Pests by Part of Naperville

  • Historic downtown and older neighborhoods. Older wood framing and mature trees make termites, carpenter ants and mice a bigger concern. A termite inspection is smart before you buy or renovate an older home.
  • Along the DuPage River and Riverwalk. Moisture and shade bring mosquitoes and ants.
  • Near Springbrook Prairie, McDowell Grove and Greene Valley. Ticks, mosquitoes and mice are more likely close to open, natural land.
  • Newer subdivisions. Ants, spiders and fall invaders like stink bugs, boxelder bugs and lady beetles are common calls. Wasp nests also start high in the eaves of taller homes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do older Naperville homes need different pest control than newer ones?

Yes. Older homes near downtown are more likely to have termite and carpenter ant risks and gaps that let mice in. Newer homes see more ants, spiders and fall invaders on sunny walls. We start with what your home and lot need.

I live near a forest preserve in Naperville. Do I need tick control?

It is worth considering. Ticks live in leaf litter, tall grass and wooded edges, and yards close to preserves like Springbrook Prairie, McDowell Grove and Greene Valley can see more of them. See our Tick Control page for how yard treatment works.

What pests are common near the DuPage River?

Mosquitoes are the main one, since they breed in standing water and rest in shade. Ants are also common in damp landscaping. Clearing standing water and treating shady resting areas helps.

When should I schedule a termite inspection in Naperville?

Late winter or early spring, before swarm season, is a good time. An inspection is also smart when you are buying an older home or planning a renovation.

Why do stink bugs and boxelder bugs cover my house in fall?

They gather on sunny walls looking for a warm place to spend winter, then slip in through small gaps. Exterior treatment in late summer and sealing gaps works better than waiting until they are inside.
