## Built on Deep Roots

Pierce Downer is one of the oldest settled parts of the village, and its housing reflects that history: a mix of original bungalows, mid century homes and newer teardown rebuilds sitting side by side on the same gridded blocks. Deep lots and a mature tree canopy are a hallmark of the area.

**Original bungalows and older homes** often have aging foundations, older wood framing and small gaps that have developed over many decades, all of which give termites, carpenter ants and mice an easier way in.

**Deep, tree canopied lots** shelter carpenter ants, ticks and spiders, and shade helps mosquitoes rest during the day.

**A mix of old and new construction on the same block** means pest pressure can vary house to house, so what your neighbor deals with is not always what you will see.
