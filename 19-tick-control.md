## The Ticks Found Around Downers Grove

**Blacklegged ticks (deer ticks).** Small and dark, they can carry the bacteria that cause Lyme disease. Nymphs, which are about the size of a poppy seed, are active in late spring and summer and are the hardest to spot. Adults are active in fall and can even appear on mild winter days.

**American dog ticks.** Larger, brown with white markings. They are common in grassy and brushy areas in spring and summer. They can carry other tick borne illnesses.

Different ticks carry different germs, and a tick bite does not always mean illness. Still, it is best to avoid bites and to remove ticks quickly.

## Where Ticks Live in Your Yard

Ticks do not live in the middle of a sunny, well mown lawn. They wait in cool, damp, shady spots and climb onto people and pets who brush past.

* Leaf litter and piles of yard debris
* Tall grass and brush at the edges of the lawn
* The line where lawn meets woods
* Ground cover, ivy and dense shrubs
* Stone walls and woodpiles, where mice and other hosts hide
* Play areas next to trees or shrubs

Ticks travel on deer, mice, chipmunks, birds and pets, so yards near trees, parks or forest preserves see more of them.

## What You Can Do to Lower Tick Risk

* Rake and remove leaf litter, especially along fences, woods and shrubs
* Mow the lawn regularly and trim tall grass at the edges
* Put a strip of wood chips or gravel between the lawn and woods
* Move play equipment, patios and seating away from wooded edges
* Stack firewood neatly and away from the house
* Keep bird feeders away from the house, since they draw mice and deer
* Talk to your vet about tick prevention for pets

## Tick Checks and Safety

* Check yourself, children and pets after being outside, especially the scalp, behind the ears, armpits, waist, groin and behind the knees
* Shower soon after coming indoors
* Dry clothes on high heat for about 10 minutes to kill ticks
* Use an EPA registered repellent and wear light colored clothes so ticks are easier to see

**If you find a tick:** Use thin pointed tweezers to grab it close to the skin and pull straight up with steady pressure. Clean the area with soap and water. Do not use petroleum jelly, a hot match or other home tricks. If you develop a rash, fever or other symptoms after a bite, contact your doctor. We are a pest control company and cannot give medical advice.

## When Ticks Are Active

Tick activity changes with the season. Nymphs are most active from late spring into summer, and adult blacklegged ticks are active in fall. On mild winter days when the ground is not frozen, adult ticks can still be found. Dog ticks are more common in spring and early summer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are ticks really a problem in Downers Grove?**
Yes. Ticks are found across the Chicago suburbs, especially around wooded areas, parks and yards with leaf litter or tall grass. Blacklegged ticks, which can carry Lyme disease, are present in this part of Illinois.

**How do ticks get into my yard?**
They arrive on deer, mice, birds and other wildlife, and on pets that have been outdoors. They then settle in cool, damp, shady spots in leaf litter, brush and wooded edges.

**Does treating the yard get rid of all ticks?**
No treatment removes every tick, and wildlife keeps bringing new ones. Treatment and yard care lower the numbers in the areas where you and your pets spend time.

**How often should my yard be treated for ticks?**
It depends on your yard and how close it is to wooded areas. Your technician will suggest a schedule based on what they find.

**Is tick treatment safe for children and pets?**
Your technician follows the product label and tells you where products were applied and how long to keep children and pets off treated areas. Ask about any concerns at the time of service.

**Can ticks live inside my home?**
Ticks usually do not live and breed indoors, but they can be carried in on pets and clothing. Check pets and family members when they come inside.

**Should I still check for ticks after the yard is treated?**
Yes. Tick checks are important whenever you are outdoors, especially in woods and tall grass, even if your yard has been treated.
