Condos, Townhomes and Houses Side by Side

Fairview is a station area, which means it holds more multi unit housing than many other parts of the village: condo buildings and townhome developments sit close to the platform, with single family blocks spreading out from there. That mix means the pest problems you deal with can depend heavily on which type of home you live in.

Condos and townhomes near the station can see cockroaches, bed bugs and mice travel between units through shared walls and floors, so the source of a problem is not always inside your own unit.

Rental turnover near a train stop means new residents and their belongings move in and out often, which is one of the more common ways bed bugs get introduced to a building.

Single family homes a few blocks from the station deal with more of the typical yard and foundation issues: ants, spiders, mice and seasonal invaders.

Frequently Asked Questions

I live in a condo near Fairview Station. Can pests come from another unit?

Yes. Cockroaches, bed bugs and mice can travel through shared walls, floors and pipe chases in condo and townhome buildings, so a problem may start somewhere else in the building. See Cockroach Control.

Why are bed bugs more common near a train station?

Areas with more rental turnover and commuter traffic tend to see more bed bug introductions, since bed bugs travel in luggage, furniture and belongings as people move. See Bed Bug Treatment.

Do single family homes near Fairview deal with different pests than the condos?

Often yes. Houses tend to see more yard and foundation related pests like ants, spiders and mice, while condos and townhomes see more shared wall issues like cockroaches and bed bugs.
