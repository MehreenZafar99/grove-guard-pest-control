## What We Focus On in Each Season

Downers Grove has four very different pest seasons. Each visit is timed to the pests that are active or about to be.

**Spring:** Ants start moving as the ground warms. Spiders come out of hiding, and carpenter ants become active. This is also termite swarm season, so we watch for signs around your foundation.

**Summer:** Wasps and hornets build nests under eaves and on decks. Ants and spiders are at their peak. We treat the outside of your home and clear small nests we can safely reach.

**Fall:** Mice and rats look for a warm place to spend the winter. Stink bugs, boxelder bugs and lady beetles gather on the sunny sides of houses. Yellow jackets are at their most aggressive. This visit builds a barrier and checks for gaps.

**Winter:** We check the home for signs of rodents and pests that survive indoors, such as cockroaches and silverfish, and look for gaps that opened up over the cold months.

## Why Quarterly Visits Work

Pest treatments do not last forever. Sun, rain and cold break them down. Pests also change with the season, so what works in spring does not always stop the pests of fall.

Regular visits keep a protective barrier in place while pests are most active. They also mean a trained person sees your home four times a year, which helps catch small issues before they become big ones.
