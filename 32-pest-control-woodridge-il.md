## A Village Built in a Hurry

Woodridge was incorporated in 1959 with fewer than 500 residents. It began on wooded high ground overlooking the East Branch of the DuPage River, and many of the first homes were bought by veterans using GI Bill loans. Larger developments followed, including Winston Hills in 1964 and Woodridge Center, which was annexed in 1972. Today the village has about 34,000 residents.

Most Woodridge homes were built from the late 1950s onward, with a lot of building in the 1960s and 1970s. Homes of that age often have worn door sweeps, aging weatherstripping, gaps around pipes and vents, and foundations that have settled. Those small openings are exactly what mice and ants look for.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why do mice get into Woodridge homes so easily?**
Many homes in the village are decades old, and worn seals, small foundation cracks and gaps around pipes and vents add up. Mice can fit through an opening about a quarter inch wide. Sealing entry points along with treatment is the key. See [Rodent Control](/rodent-control-downers-grove-il).

**I live in a Woodridge condo. Can pests come from my neighbors?**
Yes. Cockroaches, bed bugs and mice can travel through shared walls, pipes and ceilings. That is why we look for the source and the path, not only the room where you see the pests.

**Do ticks live in Woodridge yards?**
They can, especially in yards near trails, forest preserves, wooded edges and leaf litter. Ticks wait in cool, shady, damp areas, not in the middle of a sunny lawn.

**When are mosquitoes worst in Woodridge?**
Mosquitoes build from late spring and are heaviest in summer, especially after heavy rain. Dumping standing water and treating shady resting areas helps keep them down.

**What should I do about ants in my Woodridge kitchen?**
Do not spray them. Sprays can scatter some colonies and make the problem worse. Call us so we can identify the ant and treat the nest. See [Ant Control](/ant-control-downers-grove-il).
