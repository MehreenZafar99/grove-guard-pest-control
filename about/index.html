How We Work

We find the cause first. Spraying without knowing why pests are there only gives short relief. We look for how they got in, what they are eating, and where they are hiding.

We match the treatment to the pest. A wasp nest, a mouse problem and a termite risk each need a different plan. We do not use one treatment for everything.

We tell you the price before we start. You will know what the service includes and what it costs before any work begins.

We follow up. Some pests need more than one visit. We tell you what to expect and check that the problem is gone.

Why Downers Grove Homes Need Local Knowledge

Downers Grove has many older homes, mature trees, basements, attached garages and homes near wooded areas. Each of these can give pests a way in or a place to live.

Illinois seasons also decide what shows up and when. Ants and termite swarms come in spring. Wasps, mosquitoes and ticks are active in summer. Yellow jackets and stink bugs appear in fall, and mice and rats move indoors as the weather turns cold. We plan our services around this calendar.
