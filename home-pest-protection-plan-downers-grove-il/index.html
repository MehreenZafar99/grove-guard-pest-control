Home Pest Protection Plan: Quarterly Pest Control in Downers Grove, IL

Pests change with the seasons, and so should your protection. With our plan, a technician visits your home four times a year to treat the outside, check for new activity and stop problems before you ever see them.

Is This Plan Right for You?

A single visit fixes the problem you have today. A plan protects you from the one coming next season.

A plan is a good fit if:

  • Ants, spiders or other pests keep coming back every year
  • You have an older home with gaps and cracks pests can use
  • You live near trees, a park or a wooded lot
  • You want fewer surprises and fewer calls to a pest company
  • You have children or pets and would rather stop pests outside than treat indoors

A single visit may be enough if:

  • You have one specific problem, such as a wasp nest by the front door
  • The problem has a clear cause that has been fixed

Not sure? Call us and we will suggest what makes sense for your home.

What We Focus On in Each Season

Downers Grove has four very different pest seasons. Each visit is timed to the pests that are active or about to be.

Spring: Ants start moving as the ground warms. Spiders come out of hiding, and carpenter ants become active. This is also termite swarm season, so we watch for signs around your foundation.

Summer: Wasps and hornets build nests under eaves and on decks. Ants and spiders are at their peak. We treat the outside of your home and clear small nests we can safely reach.

Fall: Mice and rats look for a warm place to spend the winter. Stink bugs, boxelder bugs and lady beetles gather on the sunny sides of houses. Yellow jackets are at their most aggressive. This visit builds a barrier and checks for gaps.

Winter: We check the home for signs of rodents and pests that survive indoors, such as cockroaches and silverfish, and look for gaps that opened up over the cold months.

Some problems need their own service. These are separate from the plan:

  • Termite Control
  • Bed Bug Treatment
  • Mosquito Control and Tick Control for the yard
  • Squirrels, raccoons, bats and other wildlife

What to Expect From Your Visits

The first visit is the longest. The technician inspects your home inside and out, treats the outside and talks with you about what you have been seeing. This can take up to an hour or more, depending on the size of your home and yard.

Later visits are shorter. Regular visits mainly cover the outside of the home and usually take less time.

You may not need to be home. If the technician can reach your yard and the exterior of the house, you can stay out. For indoor treatment, someone needs to let the technician in.

Why Quarterly Visits Work

Pest treatments do not last forever. Sun, rain and cold break them down. Pests also change with the season, so what works in spring does not always stop the pests of fall.

Regular visits keep a protective barrier in place while pests are most active. They also mean a trained person sees your home four times a year, which helps catch small issues before they become big ones.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the plan cost?

The price depends on the size of your home and yard, the pests you deal with and how often we visit. We give a free estimate and explain what is included before you sign up.

How often will you visit?

We visit four times a year, once each season. Your technician will schedule your next visit before leaving so you know when to expect us.

Do I need to be home for each visit?

Not for outside treatments as long as we can get to the yard. For indoor treatments, someone needs to be home.

Will the plan get rid of mice?

The plan helps keep mice out by checking for gaps and treating the outside of your home. If you already have mice inside, you will need Mouse Control to remove them first.

What if pests come back between visits?

Call us. If pests covered by your plan return, we will come back and treat again at no extra charge.

Is there a contract?

Call us and we will explain how the plan works.

Is the treatment safe for children and pets?

Treatments follow product labels and safety rules. Most of the work happens outside, and your technician will tell you what was used and if anything needs to be avoided for a short time.
