Frequently Asked Questions

What pest control services do you offer in Downers Grove?

We treat ants, carpenter ants, termites, mice, rats, bed bugs, cockroaches, spiders, wasps, hornets, yellow jackets, mosquitoes, ticks, fleas and seasonal pests like boxelder bugs, stink bugs and silverfish. We also offer a year round protection plan.

Do I need a plan, or can I book a single visit?

Both are available. A single visit works for a specific problem like a wasp nest. A protection plan is better if you want to keep pests out through every season.

Which pest is the most common in Downers Grove?

Ants, mice and spiders are among the most common calls. Termites and carpenter ants are also a concern because of the older homes and mature trees in the area. Wasps and yellow jackets peak in summer and fall.

Can you treat more than one pest at the same time?

Yes. Many homes have more than one issue, such as ants and spiders. We can address them in the same visit when it makes sense.

Do you treat commercial buildings?

Yes. We help with homes and businesses, including restaurants, offices, shops, rentals and warehouses. Tell us the type of property when you call or on the form.

Are your treatments safe for children and pets?

Treatments follow product labels and safety rules. Your technician will tell you what was used and if anything must be avoided for a short time after service. Ask about lower risk options if you have young children or pets.
