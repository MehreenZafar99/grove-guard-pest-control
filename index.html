Why pests are common in Downers Grove homes

Downers Grove has many established neighborhoods with older homes, mature trees, basements and attached garages. Together they give pests plenty of ways in and plenty of places to hide: gaps in foundations, attics, crawl spaces and wood that touches the soil. Across the village's 14.65 square miles, the typical home was built around 1974, and brick, vinyl and aluminum exteriors can each have seams and gaps that need checking.

Illinois weather adds to it. Cold winters push mice and rats indoors. Wet springs bring termite swarms and carpenter ants. Warm months bring wasps, mosquitoes and ticks. A treatment that works in July will not stop a mouse problem in October, so we match the service to the pest and the season.

How much does pest control cost in Downers Grove?

The price depends on the pest, the size of your home and yard, and how many visits it takes. A single treatment costs less than a year round plan. We give a free estimate and explain the price before any work starts.

How often should I have pest control?

If you have one specific problem, like a wasp nest, a single visit may be enough. To keep pests out all year, most homes do best with regular visits through the seasons, since each season brings different pests.

When do termites swarm in Illinois?

Termites usually swarm in spring, often in April and May after warm rain. Winged insects or shed wings near windows and doors are a warning sign. Call us for an inspection right away.

Why do I see mice in my house every fall?

Falling temperatures push mice indoors to find warmth and food. They can squeeze through very small gaps in foundations, garages and around pipes. Sealing entry points along with treatment is the best way to stop them.

Do I need to be home during service?

Not always. Exterior treatments can often be done while you are away. For indoor work, someone needs to be home so the technician can get inside.

Do you treat businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We help homeowners, renters, landlords and businesses such as restaurants, offices, shops and warehouses. Tell us the type of property on the form or when you call.

Is pest control safe for children and pets?

Treatments follow product labels and safety rules. Your technician explains what was used, where it was applied and how long to keep children and pets away from treated areas. Tell us about children and pets before service so we can plan around them.
