## An Old Village With Deep Roots

Settlers claimed land near the East Branch of the DuPage River in the 1830s, in a large stand of timber that became known as Babcock's Grove. Glen Ellyn was incorporated in 1896 and today has about 29,000 residents. It began as a turn of the century resort community, and the village still has a walkable historic downtown, and a Historic Preservation Commission that helps protect older landmarks.

Many neighborhoods were built when wood framing, front porches, wood trim and large shade trees were standard. Newer homes have been built among them, so a single block can hold houses of very different ages.

## Parks, Trails and Lake Ellyn

The Glen Ellyn Park District, founded in 1919, looks after about 30 parks, including Lake Ellyn Park. The Illinois Prairie Path and the Great Western Trail pass through the area as green corridors.

Water and greenery bring their own pests. Lake Ellyn and shaded yards nearby can mean more mosquitoes, and brushy, wooded stretches along trails and parks can shelter ticks.

## What Glen Ellyn's Older Homes Deal With

* **Termites.** Older wood framing, wood close to soil and past moisture problems raise the risk. A termite inspection is a smart step before you buy or renovate.
* **Carpenter ants.** Large shade trees and damp wood near windows, porches and roofs give them places to nest.
* **Carpenter bees.** Porch railings, eaves, pergolas and weathered trim are prime targets.
* **Mice.** Older foundations, garage doors and gaps around pipes and vents let them in each fall.
* **Silverfish.** Humid basements with stored paper and fabrics are ideal.
* **Mosquitoes and ticks.** Yards close to Lake Ellyn, parks and trails may see more of both.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are historic Glen Ellyn homes more likely to have termites?**
Older homes with wood framing, basements or crawl spaces and moisture problems can be at higher risk. Termites can hide for a long time, so a professional inspection before you buy, sell or renovate is worth it. See [Termite Inspection](/termite-inspection-downers-grove-il).

**Why are carpenter bees drilling into my porch?**
Carpenter bees prefer bare or weathered softwood, and many older homes have wood porches, railings and trim. Treating the tunnels, sealing the holes after the bees are gone and painting or staining the wood helps. See [Carpenter Bee Control](/carpenter-bee-control-downers-grove-il).

**Do big shade trees cause carpenter ants?**
Trees do not cause them, but dead limbs, stumps and damp wood can host nests, and carpenter ants often travel from a tree to a nearby house. Large ants after dark or sawdust piles near windows are warning signs. See [Carpenter Ant Control](/carpenter-ant-control-downers-grove-il).

**Do ticks live near the Prairie Path and parks?**
They can. Ticks wait in leaf litter, tall grass and brushy edges, and yards that touch parks or trails may see more. Check yourself and your pets after time outdoors. See [Tick Control](/tick-control-downers-grove-il).

**Are mosquitoes worse near Lake Ellyn?**
Water and shady plants help mosquitoes breed and rest, so yards near the lake and other water may see more of them. Dump standing water weekly and consider treating shaded resting areas.
