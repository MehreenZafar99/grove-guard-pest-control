Pest Control in Darien, IL

The entire south edge of Darien borders Waterfall Glen Forest Preserve, a large area of woods, prairie and creeks. It is a beautiful neighbor, and it also sends ticks, mosquitoes, rodents and other pests toward nearby yards. We treat Darien homes with the preserve in mind.

The Preserve Next Door

Waterfall Glen covers nearly 2,500 acres of oak savanna, woodland and prairie, with Sawmill Creek and the Rocky Glen waterfall. Its woods and grassland shelter a huge range of plants, birds, mammals and insects, and it lies directly along the southern edge of Darien.

That wildlife does not stay inside the preserve. Mice, rats, chipmunks and other small animals move into nearby yards and homes, especially in fall. Ticks ride in on deer, mice and other animals and wait in leaf litter and tall grass at wooded edges. Mosquitoes breed in damp ground and creeks and rest in shaded brush.

Established Neighborhoods, Mature Trees

Darien was incorporated in 1969, and its neighborhoods, including Marion Hills, Brookhaven, Clairfield and Hinsbrook, are well established. Many homes have mature trees and landscaped yards. Older trees can host carpenter ants, and dense landscaping gives spiders, earwigs and wasps places to build and hide.

What Homes Near Waterfall Glen Deal With

  • Ticks. Yards that touch wooded edges, leaf litter and tall grass see more of them. Pets can carry ticks and fleas from the yard into the house.
  • Mosquitoes. Damp ground, creeks and shady shrubs give mosquitoes a place to breed and rest.
  • Mice and rats. Fall brings them toward warm homes, garages and sheds.
  • Fleas. Wildlife passing through yards can leave fleas under decks, porches and sheds.
  • Spiders and carpenter ants. Wooded lots and mature trees give both plenty of shelter.
  • Wasps and hornets. Nests can appear in trees, shrubs, sheds and under eaves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does living near Waterfall Glen mean more pests?

It can. Wooded and grassy areas shelter ticks, mosquitoes, rodents and other wildlife, and some of them move into nearby yards. Homes along the edge of the preserve may see more, but treatment and yard care can bring numbers down.

How do I protect my family and pets from ticks in Darien?

Keep grass short, rake leaf litter and trim brush at the edge of the yard. Check people and pets after time outdoors, and talk to your vet about tick prevention. Yard treatment focused on the wooded edge and shady areas lowers tick numbers. See Tick Control.

Why do mice come into my Darien home in fall?

Falling temperatures push mice and rats indoors to find warmth and food, and homes near wooded land see them often. Sealing gaps around the foundation, garage and utility lines is a key step. See Mouse Control.

Can wildlife bring fleas to my yard?

Yes. Animals like opossums, raccoons and stray cats can leave fleas under decks, porches and sheds. If you have pets, treating the yard and the home along with your pet's flea prevention works best. See Flea Control.

When should I start mosquito control in Darien?

Late spring, before mosquito numbers build. Dump standing water weekly and consider treating shady spots where mosquitoes rest.
